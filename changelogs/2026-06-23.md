# Changelog — June 23, 2026

## New Models

### MiniMax M3

MiniMax's flagship M3 model ([model card](https://www.minimax.io/models/text/m3)) is the first open-weight model to deliver frontier coding, a 1M-token context window, and native multimodality in a single architecture.

On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1, M3 scores 44 — placing alongside DeepSeek V4-Pro and ahead of Kimi K2.6 among open-weight models ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/minimax-m3)). On SWE-Bench Pro it achieves 59.0%, surpassing GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro and approaching Opus 4.7 ([MiniMax technical blog](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m3)). Terminal-Bench 2.1 comes in at 66.0%, and BrowseComp at 83.5, beating Claude Opus 4.7's 79.3. On GDPval-AA, a real-world task benchmark across 44 occupations, M3 scores approximately 1670 — level with Claude Sonnet 4.6 ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/minimax-m3)).

The core innovation is MiniMax Sparse Attention (MSA), which replaces full quadratic attention with a lightweight index that scans tokens and selects only the relevant context blocks. At 1M tokens, MSA cuts per-token compute to one-twentieth of the prior generation, with over 9× faster prefill and over 15× faster decoding ([arXiv:2606.13392](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13392)). The model is natively multimodal from step zero, trained on roughly 100 trillion interleaved text, image, and video tokens — not patched on after the fact.

Pricing is $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens (permanent 50% promotional discount on the standard $0.60/$2.40 rate), making M3 one of the most cost-effective frontier models available ([MiniMax pricing](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/pricing-paygo)). Open weights are planned but not yet released; MiniMax has stated they will follow within days of launch.

## Status Changes

### Claude Fable 5 — Suspended

Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 remain suspended for all users worldwide, eleven days after the US Commerce Department issued an export control directive on June 12, 2026 ([Anthropic statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access)). The order, citing national security authorities, required Anthropic to disable the two Mythos-class models for all customers globally. Anthropic has stated it 'disagrees that the finding of a narrow potential jailbreak should be cause for recalling a commercial model' but is complying while negotiating a resolution ([BBC coverage](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c932g3v3e13o)).

Anthropic's Managing Director of International Chris Ciauri said on June 17 in Seoul that the models will be available again 'in the coming days' ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.totalum.app/blog/claude-fable-5-restoration-update-2026)), but no firm date has been published. As of today, status.claude.com still shows the incident as 'Monitoring.' Claude Opus 4.8, Sonnet 4.6, and Haiku 4.5 remain unaffected.
