# Changelog — May 1, 2026

## Industry News

### OpenAI Announces GPT-5.5-Cyber for Trusted Defenders
OpenAI announced GPT-5.5-Cyber, a specialized cybersecurity model restricted to "critical cyber defenders" through the Trusted Access for Cyber (TAC) program. The model will not be publicly available. Sam Altman stated rollout begins "in the next few days" with government coordination. This follows Anthropic's restricted release of Claude Mythos Preview earlier in April. https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/921073/openai-sam-altman-new-cybersecurity-model-gpt-5-5-cyber

### UK AISI Evaluates GPT-5.5 Cyber Capabilities
The UK AI Security Institute published evaluations of GPT-5.5's cyber capabilities. Key findings: GPT-5.5 achieved 71.4% (±8.0%) on Expert-level cyber tasks, compared to 68.6% for Mythos Preview, 52.4% for GPT-5.4, and 48.6% for Opus 4.7. GPT-5.5 became the second model to solve "The Last Ones" (TLO) end-to-end, a 32-step corporate network attack simulation estimated to take a human ~20 hours. GPT-5.5 solved TLO in 2 of 10 attempts; Mythos Preview solved it in 3 of 10. https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/our-evaluation-of-openais-gpt-5-5-cyber-capabilities

### Pentagon Signs Classified AI Deals
The Pentagon reached agreements with Google, OpenAI, and xAI for classified AI deployments. Google's Gemini will be used for classified military work. Anthropic remains excluded from DOD contracts after refusing to remove guardrails on autonomous weapons and mass surveillance. Approximately 700 Google employees signed a letter opposing the deal. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/28/pentagon-ai-chief-confirms-work-with-google-after-anthropic-blacklist.html

### Meta Releases Llama 5
Meta released Llama 5 (~April 15, 2026) as a 600B+ parameter open-weights multimodal model with a 1M token context window. Meta claims "recursive self-improvement" capabilities allowing inference-time weight refinement, though this has not been independently verified. The Epoch AI evaluation URL was reportedly broken at publication time. https://techjacksolutions.com/ai-brief/meta-llama-5-a-600b-parameter-open-weights-model-an/
