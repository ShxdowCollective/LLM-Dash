# Changelog — April 26, 2026

## New Models

### GPT-5.5
OpenAI's new frontier model released April 23, 2026. Achieves **82.7% on Terminal-Bench 2.0**, establishing a new state-of-the-art on agentic coding workflows. SWE-Bench Pro: 58.6%. OSWorld-Verified: 78.7%. GPQA Diamond: 93.6%. GDPval: 84.9% (17.6 pts ahead of Gemini 3.1 Pro). The model matches GPT-5.4 per-token latency while delivering significantly higher intelligence. Notable gains in scientific research workflows (GeneBench 25.0% vs GPT-5.4's 19.0%). Pricing: $5/$30 per 1M tokens. API availability staggered following ChatGPT rollout. [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/)

### GPT-5.5 Pro
Higher-accuracy variant using parallel test-time compute on the GPT-5.5 base. FrontierMath Tier 4: 39.6% (vs base's 35.4%). BrowseComp: 90.1% (vs base's 84.4%). GPQA Diamond: 94.4%. HLE with tools: 57.2%. Targeted at the hardest reasoning tasks. Premium pricing: $30/$180 per 1M tokens. Available to Pro, Business, and Enterprise users in ChatGPT. [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/)

### DeepSeek V4 Pro Max
DeepSeek open-sourced their V4 frontier model under MIT license on April 23-24, 2026. 1.6T total parameters with 49B active per forward pass. **80.6% SWE-bench Verified** — highest published score for open-weight models. LiveCodeBench: 93.5%. Codeforces ELO: 3206 (ranked 23rd among human competitors). Terminal-Bench 2.0: 67.9%. GPQA Diamond: 90.1%. 1M context window. Pricing: ~$1.74/$3.48 per 1M tokens via DeepSeek API. MIT license enables unrestricted commercial use. [Source](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash)

### DeepSeek V4 Flash Max
Efficient variant of V4 with 284B total parameters and 13B active. SWE-bench Verified: 79.0% (1.6 pts behind Pro Max). GPQA Diamond: 88.1%. LiveCodeBench: 91.6%. 10x cheaper than Pro Max at ~$0.28/$0.56 per 1M tokens. Strong performance-per-dollar for production workloads not requiring frontier reasoning. MIT license. [Source](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash)

### Qwen3.6-27B
Alibaba released a 27B dense open-weight model April 21, 2026 that **outperforms its 397B MoE predecessor on coding benchmarks**. SWE-bench Verified: 77.2% (vs Qwen3.5-397B's 76.2%). Terminal-Bench 2.0: 59.3% (matches Claude 4.5 Opus exactly). SWE-bench Pro: 53.5% (exceeds 397B MoE's 50.9%). LiveCodeBench v6: 83.9%. GPQA Diamond: 87.8%. Apache 2.0 license. Pricing: ~$0.10/$0.20 per 1M tokens. [Source](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B)

### Kimi K2.6
Moonshot AI moved K2.6 from preview to GA on April 21, 2026 — just 8 days after preview announcement. The model now supports **12-hour agent runs** and **300-agent swarm workflows**. Full-stack generation capability for enterprise production deployments. Builds on the K2.5 Thinking foundation with improved long-horizon coding. Pricing: ~$0.60/$3.00 per 1M tokens. [Source](https://kimi-k2.org/blog/24-kimi-k2-6-release)
